# African Startup Partnership Intelligence

## Project Overview

African Startup Partnership Intelligence is a research and data analysis project conducted during my internship with AFRIMENT to investigate prospective organisations and evaluate their potential partnership opportunities with AFRIMENT.

The project assessed observable indicators such as hiring activity, entry-level opportunities, remote opportunities, internship programmes, relevant programmes, industry alignment and geographic reach.

To structure the assessment, I developed the **Employer Compatibility Index (ECI)** — a framework designed to measure how compatible a prospect was with AFRIMENT's partnership objectives and support the evaluation and prioritisation of potential partnership opportunities.

The results were presented through a dashboard to support prospect comparison and partnership prioritisation.

## Research Focus

The research focused on identifying organisations that showed observable signals of potential compatibility with AFRIMENT's partnership objectives.

The analysis considered:

- Hiring activity
- Entry-level opportunities
- Remote opportunities
- Internship opportunities
- Programmes offered by prospects
- Alignment between prospect programmes and AFRIMENT programmes
- Geographic reach
- Industry alignment

These indicators were combined within the ECI framework to produce component scores, an overall score and partnership-priority tiers.

## Data Collection

Information was collected through desk research using publicly available online sources.

Sources included company websites, LinkedIn company and employee pages, job boards, career pages, internship and programme pages, search engines and other relevant online sources.

The research focused on information that could provide observable evidence of hiring activity, programme offerings, internship opportunities, geographic reach and other indicators relevant to potential partnership compatibility.

The information gathered was organised into a structured dataset and used as the basis for the ECI assessment.

## Data Structure

The dataset contained information relating to selected startups across African countries and industries.

Key variables included:

- **Country** — country associated with the prospect.
- **Industry** — industry in which the prospect operates.
- **Total Job Postings** — observed job postings associated with the prospect.
- **Entry-Level Jobs** — observed entry-level opportunities.
- **Remote Jobs** — observed remote opportunities.
- **Total Programs** — total relevant programmes identified as being offered by the prospect.
- **Matching Programs** — programmes offered by the prospect that appeared to align with programmes already available through AFRIMENT's bootcamp or internship cohorts.
- **Internship Programs** — whether the prospect was identified as offering internship opportunities.
- **Hiring Countries** — geographic extent of the prospect's hiring activity.
- **Hiring Score** — score derived from observed hiring activity.
- **Entry Score** — score representing observed entry-level opportunities.
- **Alignment Score** — score representing programme alignment between the prospect and AFRIMENT.
- **Remote Score** — score representing observed remote opportunities.
- **Internship Score** — score representing internship opportunities.
- **Geography Score** — score representing geographic reach.
- **Total Score** — combined score used to assess overall compatibility.
- **Tier** — priority classification based on the total score.

## Employer Compatibility Index (ECI)

The **Employer Compatibility Index (ECI)** was developed for this project to provide a structured approach for assessing potential partnership compatibility.

Rather than relying on a single indicator, the framework considered multiple dimensions of a prospect's activities and opportunities.

The assessment incorporated:

- Hiring activity
- Entry-level opportunities
- Programme alignment
- Remote opportunities
- Internship opportunities
- Geographic reach

These dimensions were converted into component scores and combined into an overall score. Prospects were then classified into priority tiers based on their total scores.

## Analysis

The analysis was used to compare prospects across different dimensions of potential partnership compatibility.

The dashboard presents the resulting analysis through views covering:

- Partnership potential
- Industry partnership opportunities
- Internship and entry-level opportunities
- Geographic reach
- Compatibility and priority tiers

This allowed the research to move beyond simply identifying potential prospects and toward a more structured approach to partnership prioritisation.

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides a visual summary of the ECI analysis and allows prospects to be compared across key partnership indicators.

It highlights patterns in hiring activity, programme alignment, internship opportunities, industry representation and geographic reach.

## Key Outcome

The project produced:

- A structured dataset of selected African startup prospects
- A multi-dimensional partnership assessment framework
- The Employer Compatibility Index (ECI)
- Component and overall compatibility scores
- Partnership-priority tiers
- A dashboard for comparing and prioritising prospects

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrated how publicly available research data can be structured and analysed to support partnership intelligence and prospect prioritisation.

It also provided a practical framework for moving from broad prospect research to a more systematic assessment of potential partnership opportunities.

## Limitations

The analysis was based on publicly available information identified during the research period.

The absence of publicly available evidence for a particular indicator does not necessarily mean that the activity does not exist. It only means that sufficient observable evidence was not identified during the research.

The ECI was developed specifically for this project as a structured assessment framework and should therefore be interpreted within the context of the AFRIMENT partnership research.
