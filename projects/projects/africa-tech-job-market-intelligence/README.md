# Africa Tech Job Market Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project analyzes technology job opportunities across Africa to identify patterns in employer demand, in-demand roles, required skills, geographic distribution, industries, remote work opportunities, and emerging technology areas.

The analysis was designed to provide evidence that can help technology training providers better understand the skills and roles employers are seeking in the African job market.

## Research Focus

The project focused on technology-related job opportunities that were open to African candidates and associated with African countries.

The analysis explored:

- Technology roles in demand
- Skills requested by employers
- Geographic distribution of opportunities
- Industry representation
- Remote and non-remote opportunities
- Emerging technology areas
- Implications for technology training and workforce preparation
  

## Data Collection

Job-posting data was collected from publicly available sources, including online job boards such as Indeed and JobMag, company career websites, and other relevant job-posting sources.

The research focused specifically on technology-related opportunities open to African candidates and associated with African countries.

The final dataset contains 160 verified job postings from 129 employers across 13 African countries and 24 industries.

## Data Preparation

The collected job postings were organized into a structured dataset for analysis.

The preparation process involved reviewing the collected records, organizing relevant job-market attributes, checking the dataset for inconsistencies, and preparing the data for analysis and visualization.

The cleaned dataset was then used to support the analysis and dashboard development.

## Analytical Approach

The analysis was structured around several dimensions of the African technology job market:

- Job-role demand
- Employer-required skills
- Geographic distribution
- Industry representation
- Remote and non-remote opportunities
- Emerging technology areas

These dimensions were analyzed to identify patterns in employer demand and areas where technology training and workforce preparation could better align with the job market.


## Analysis

The analysis examined patterns across:

- Job roles
- Employer-required skills
- Countries
- Industries
- Work arrangements
- Emerging technology areas

The findings were then translated into visual summaries through the accompanying dashboard.

## Dashboard

The dashboard presents the major patterns identified in the job-market dataset and allows the findings to be interpreted from different perspectives, including role demand, skills, geography, industry and work arrangement.

## Key Findings

Key findings from the analysis are presented in the accompanying dashboard.

The dashboard was used to identify patterns in employer demand and highlight areas that may be relevant to technology training, curriculum development and workforce preparation.

## Business Relevance

The findings can help training providers and other workforce-development stakeholders better understand the relationship between:

**Employer demand → Required skills → Training priorities**

This provides a data-informed basis for evaluating whether existing training programmes align with the skills and roles appearing in the job market.

## Limitations

The analysis is based on publicly available job postings collected from selected sources. Therefore, the dataset may not represent every technology vacancy across Africa.

Job postings also represent advertised demand rather than a complete measure of the labour market or actual hiring outcomes.
