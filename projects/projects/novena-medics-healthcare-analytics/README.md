# Novena Medics — Healthcare Data Analytics

## Project Overview

Novena Medics is a healthcare data analytics case study built around a structured patient dataset containing 189 patient records.

The project explores patient demographics, geographic distribution, diagnoses, treatment types, assigned medical personnel, hospital stay duration, treatment costs, and patient outcomes.

The dataset was analysed and presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard to demonstrate how structured healthcare data can be transformed into information that supports operational reporting and decision-making.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns in patient activity, treatment, cost, length of stay, and outcomes, while demonstrating practical data analysis and visualisation techniques in a healthcare context.

## Data & Analysis Focus

The analysis focused on understanding patterns across several areas of hospital operations and patient activity.

Key areas included:

- Patient demographics and age distribution
- Patient distribution by state
- Diagnosis patterns
- Treatment types
- Patient assignment across medical personnel
- Hospital stay duration
- Treatment costs
- Patient outcomes
- Relationships between treatment cost, length of stay, and patient outcomes

These areas were explored through interactive visualisations and summary metrics in Power BI to provide a clearer view of the underlying patient data.

## Data Preparation

The patient dataset was reviewed and structured to support analysis and reporting.

The preparation process included:

- Reviewing the dataset structure and variables
- Checking data completeness
- Validating date and numerical fields
- Organising patient, treatment, cost, and outcome variables for analysis
- Preparing calculated measures such as hospital stay duration
- Structuring the data for Power BI visualisation and reporting

The prepared dataset served as the underlying data source for the Power BI dashboard.

## Analysis

The prepared dataset was analysed using Power BI to explore patient activity, treatment patterns, hospital operations, costs, and outcomes.

The dashboard included:

- Summary KPIs for patient volume, hospital stay, and treatment costs
- Patient volume and demographic analysis
- Geographic distribution of patients
- Diagnosis and treatment analysis
- Distribution of patients across doctors
- Hospital stay analysis
- Treatment cost analysis
- Patient outcome analysis
- Visual exploration of relationships between treatment cost, length of stay, and other patient-level variables

Interactive filters and visualisations were used to allow different aspects of the dataset to be explored from multiple perspectives.

## Key Insights

The dashboard provided a consolidated view of patient activity and hospital operations.

Key areas of insight included:

- The distribution of patients across different age groups and demographic categories
- Differences in patient representation across states
- The frequency of different diagnoses and treatment types
- The distribution of patients across assigned doctors
- Variation in hospital stay duration
- Differences in treatment costs across patient records and treatment categories
- The distribution of patient outcomes
- Relationships between treatment costs and hospital stay duration

The dashboard was designed to make these patterns easier to identify and interpret through interactive visual reporting.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard presents the analysis through interactive healthcare visualisations and summary metrics.

The dashboard allows users to explore patient demographics, diagnoses, treatments, doctors, hospital stays, costs, and outcomes within a single reporting environment.

The Power BI project file is included in the `dashboard` folder.

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how a structured healthcare dataset can be transformed into an interactive reporting solution for exploring patient and operational information.

The resulting dashboard provides a consolidated view of patient activity, treatment patterns, hospital stay, costs, and outcomes, demonstrating how data analysis and visualisation can support clearer reporting and operational understanding in a healthcare setting.

## Data & Privacy

Novena Medics is a fictional healthcare analytics case study created for portfolio and learning purposes.

The dataset is used to demonstrate healthcare data analysis, reporting, and visualisation techniques and should not be interpreted as a real hospital patient database.

No real patient information is intended to be represented in this project.

## Limitations

The analysis is based on the available dataset and therefore reflects only the variables and records included in the case study.

The dataset represents a portfolio case study rather than a complete hospital information system. The findings should therefore be interpreted within the scope of the available data and should not be generalised to real-world hospital populations.

Some operational relationships may also require additional clinical, financial, or administrative data before they could be used for real-world decision-making.
