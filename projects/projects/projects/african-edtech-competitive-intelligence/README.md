# African EdTech Competitive Intelligence

## Project Overview

African EdTech Competitive Intelligence is a research and data analysis project focused on understanding the African EdTech and career-support landscape and comparing selected providers across key business and programme characteristics.

The research examined areas such as programmes offered, pricing, target audiences, geographic reach, learning formats, career support, partnerships and other competitive features.

The analysis was designed to identify similarities and differences between providers, understand how programmes and pricing vary across the market, and identify gaps and potential opportunities within existing offerings.

## Research Focus

The research focused on selected African EdTech and career-support companies offering skills development, training, bootcamps, internships, career development and related employability opportunities.

The analysis examined:

- Programmes and courses offered
- Target audience
- Pricing
- Geographic reach
- Learning modalities
- Mentorship and career support
- Internship and placement opportunities
- Partnerships
- Programme differentiators

The objective was to compare how different providers positioned their offerings, identify patterns across the African EdTech landscape, and uncover gaps and potential opportunities within existing programmes.

## Data Collection

Information was collected through desk research using publicly available online sources.

Sources included company websites, LinkedIn company and employee pages, programme and course pages, pricing pages, career and internship pages, search engines and other relevant online sources.

The collected information was organised into a structured dataset and used to support the comparative analysis.

## Data Preparation

The collected information was organised into a structured dataset for comparison and analysis.

The dataset was reviewed and prepared to allow information from different EdTech and career-support providers to be examined across common characteristics, including programmes, pricing, target audiences, learning formats, career support, partnerships and geographic reach.

## Analysis

The analysis compared selected providers across multiple dimensions of their offerings and market positioning.

The analysis examined:

- Programme offerings
- Pricing structures
- Target audiences
- Geographic presence
- Learning formats
- Career and employability support
- Internship and placement opportunities
- Mentorship
- Partnerships
- Programme differentiators

The findings were presented through a dashboard to make the competitive landscape easier to compare and interpret.

## Key Areas of Insight

The research provided a basis for examining:

- How EdTech providers differ in their programme offerings
- How pricing varies across providers and programmes
- The types of learners and professionals being targeted
- Differences in learning and delivery models
- The availability of career development and employability support
- The extent of internship and placement opportunities
- Areas where existing programme offerings may have gaps
- Potential opportunities for differentiation and partnership

## Dashboard

The dashboard presents the findings from the competitive intelligence research and provides a visual comparison of selected African EdTech and career-support providers.

It brings together information on programme offerings, pricing, geographic reach, career support and other competitive characteristics to support market comparison.

## Limitations

The analysis was based on information publicly available during the research period.

Programme offerings, pricing, partnerships and other company information can change over time. The findings therefore represent the information identified during the period of research and should not be treated as a permanent representation of the African EdTech market.

The research also focused on selected providers and therefore does not represent every EdTech or career-support organisation operating across Africa.
