# Master's Research Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project involved the analysis of research data for a Master's research study examining the supply chain linkage between farming and hospitality industries in Imo State, Nigeria.

The study explored how agricultural products move between farmers and hospitality businesses, the procurement and supply-chain structures involved, challenges affecting the linkage, and factors related to infrastructure, information flow, pricing, product availability, product quality, and supply-chain coordination.

I worked as the Research Data Analyst, responsible for preparing, cleaning, validating, transforming, analysing, visualising, and interpreting the research data to support the client's research objectives and academic reporting.

The project combined data preparation, descriptive statistical analysis, qualitative response categorisation, hypothesis testing, and research insight generation to turn the collected responses into structured analytical findings.

## Research Focus

The research focused on understanding the relationship between agricultural producers and hospitality businesses within the study area.

The analysis examined:

- Agricultural products supplied to hospitality businesses
- Procurement and sourcing practices
- Supply-chain structures and coordination mechanisms
- Relationships and contractual arrangements between farmers and hospitality businesses
- Challenges affecting agricultural supply-chain linkage
- Infrastructure and logistics factors
- Communication and information flow
- Pricing and market stability
- Product availability and quality
- Digital and technological approaches to supply-chain coordination
- Potential strategies for improving supply-chain integration

The research was designed to provide evidence on the factors influencing the flow of agricultural products from farmers to hospitality businesses and the mechanisms that could strengthen this linkage.

## Data Collection

The research data was collected through structured questionnaires administered to respondents involved in the farming and hospitality sectors within Imo State.

The collected responses captured information relating to agricultural production, products supplied, procurement practices, supply-chain relationships, operational challenges, infrastructure, information flow, pricing, product availability and quality, and approaches to improving supply-chain coordination.

The resulting dataset was prepared for analysis by organising the collected responses into structured variables suitable for statistical analysis, cross-tabulation, thematic categorisation, and interpretation.

## Data Preparation

The collected research responses were prepared for analysis through a structured data preparation process.

The process included:

- Cleaning and validating the collected data
- Transforming variables into suitable analytical formats
- Categorising qualitative responses into meaningful groups
- Applying binary coding where appropriate
- Organising variables for descriptive and comparative analysis
- Preparing the dataset for frequency analysis, cross-tabulation, pivot tables, and hypothesis testing

The preparation process was focused on improving the consistency, structure, and reliability of the research dataset before analysis.

## Analysis

The research data was analysed using a combination of quantitative and qualitative analytical approaches.

The analysis included:

- Descriptive statistics using frequency counts and percentages
- Cross-tabulation of relevant variables
- Pivot-table analysis
- Thematic analysis of categorised responses
- Supply-chain mapping
- Binary coding of selected variables
- Hypothesis testing using chi-square analysis

The analysis was used to examine patterns in agricultural supply, procurement and supply-chain structures, identify challenges affecting the farming–hospitality linkage, and assess factors related to infrastructure, coordination, information flow, digital integration, and other supply-chain conditions.

The findings were subsequently interpreted in relation to the research objectives and used to support the client's academic research reporting.

## Key Findings

The analysis identified several factors affecting the linkage between farming and hospitality businesses in Imo State.

Key findings included:

- Government support, policy, and agricultural inputs were among the most frequently reported supply-chain challenges.
- Contracting, supply agreements, and trust between farmers and hospitality businesses were significant areas of concern.
- Storage and warehousing presented important constraints to effective supply-chain coordination.
- Delivery, logistics, and timing affected the movement and availability of agricultural products.
- Digital and technological tools for tracking agricultural products and sharing data with farmers showed significant gaps in current supply-chain integration.
- Infrastructure, information flow, contractual relationships, coordination, and collaboration were identified as important conditions influencing supply-chain integration.
- The statistical analysis indicated that the identified supply-chain challenges were significant rather than occurring randomly within the responses.
- The findings also highlighted opportunities to strengthen supply-chain integration through improved coordination, information sharing, digital tools, infrastructure, contractual arrangements, and collaboration among stakeholders.

## Project Outcome

The project produced a structured and validated research dataset that supported the analysis of farming–hospitality supply-chain linkages in Imo State.

The analysis provided evidence on procurement practices, supply-chain challenges, coordination mechanisms, infrastructure, information flow, digital integration, and other factors affecting the relationship between farmers and hospitality businesses.

The resulting analytical findings were used to support the client's Master's research report and provide a clearer evidence base for interpreting the study's research objectives.

## Limitations

The analysis was based on the responses collected for the research study and therefore reflects the information available within the study dataset.

The findings are specific to the study context in Imo State and should not be interpreted as representing all farming and hospitality businesses across Nigeria.

Supply-chain conditions, market relationships, infrastructure, pricing, and digital practices may also change over time. The findings therefore represent the conditions captured during the research period.
